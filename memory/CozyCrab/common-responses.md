# CozyCrab — Common Responses

## View / Location
- Cozy Crab is in an oceanfront building on the Virginia Beach boardwalk
- The unit is side-facing and does NOT have an ocean view
- Do not imply or promise an ocean view

## Pool
- The building has a shared outdoor pool — seasonal and unheated
- Usually open mid-May through mid-October, weather dependent
- Pool availability is not guaranteed; confirm with host if important

## Pets
- No pets are allowed at this property — no exceptions
- $500 fine if evidence of pets is found

## Smoking
- No smoking or vaping indoors, in the hallway, or on building premises
- $500 fine per violation

## Parties
- No events, parties, or gatherings of any kind
- $500 fine per violation

## Late Checkout
- Late checkout is not available
- Checkout is 10:00 AM; cleaners arrive at that time
- $200 fee for violation — not an approval to stay late

## Coffee
- Keurig coffee maker available

## Parking
- One assigned parking space included at no charge
- Additional vehicles must use nearby public parking

## WiFi
- High-speed WiFi is included; credentials are in the welcome guide

## Laundry
- Paid washer and dryer available in the building (coin or app operated)

## Beach & Outdoor
- Steps from the sand — beachfront building location
- Outdoor shower available
- BBQ grills on the 2nd floor

## Toiletries
- Shampoo, body soap, and shower gel are provided
