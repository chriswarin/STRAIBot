## Policy or Fact

No pets are allowed at Cozy Crab under any circumstances.

- No pets of any kind.
- $500 fine will be charged if evidence of pets is found.
- No exceptions for standard pet requests.

## Guest Response Guidance

Respond firmly and politely. State no pets, no exceptions, and mention the $500 fine.

## Suggested Response

Hi there! Unfortunately Cozy Crab does not allow pets of any kind — no exceptions. A $500 fine applies if evidence of a pet is found. We hope you can still enjoy this great spot steps from the Virginia Beach boardwalk!
