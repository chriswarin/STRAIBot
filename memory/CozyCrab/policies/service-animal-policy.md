## Policy or Fact

Service animal, ADA accommodation, emotional support animal, and legal accommodation requests require host review and notification. Do not auto-approve or auto-deny.

## Guest Response Guidance

Acknowledge request. Inform guest the host will follow up. Do not reference pet policy fines.

## Suggested Response

Thanks for reaching out. Requests of this nature need to be reviewed directly by the host. I've passed your message along and someone will follow up with you as soon as possible.

## Escalation Notes

RequiresHostReview: true, ShouldNotifyHost: true for ANY of: service animal, emotional support animal, ESA, ADA, legal accommodation, accommodation request, lawsuit, discrimination, already brought animal.
