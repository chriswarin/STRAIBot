## Policy or Fact

Cozy Crab guests have access to the shared outdoor building pool.

- Shared outdoor pool, available to all building guests.
- Unheated — ambient temperature only.
- Usually open mid-May through mid-October or May through October, weather dependent.
- Non-toilet-trained guests (including those in diapers) must wear swim diapers or rubber swim pants.
- Guests will cover additional pool cleaning, draining, or refilling costs if pool is soiled by bodily fluids or trash.
