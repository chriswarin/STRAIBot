## Policy or Fact

Cooking at Cozy Crab is welcome with restrictions.

- No heavy cooking.
- No aromatic foods.
- No gutting or cleaning fish, shellfish, or game animals inside the property.
- Everyday cooking is welcome.
- Full kitchen: electric stove, oven, microwave, refrigerator, cookware, dishes.

## Guest Response Guidance

Confirm kitchen available for everyday use. State heavy aromatic cooking and fish/game cleaning are not permitted.

## Suggested Response

Hi! Cozy Crab has a full kitchen — electric stove, oven, microwave, refrigerator, and cookware. Everyday cooking is totally welcome! We just ask guests to avoid heavy aromatic cooking or cleaning seafood/game inside the unit.
