## Policy or Fact

Primary guest must be at least 25 years old to book Cozy Crab.

- Must be at least 25 to book.
- All guests under 25 must be related to a guest who is 25 or older.
- Chaperoned stays such as sports teams or organized groups are case by case.

## Guest Response Guidance

State the 25+ requirement. Note under-25 guests are welcome if related to a 25+ guest. Escalate chaperoned group requests.

## Suggested Response

Hi! The primary guest at Cozy Crab must be 25 or older to book. Guests under 25 are welcome when traveling with a direct family member who meets the age requirement.
