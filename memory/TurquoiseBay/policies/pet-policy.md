## Policy or Fact

No pets are allowed at Turquoise Bay under any circumstances.

- No pets of any kind.
- $500 fine will be charged if evidence of pets is found.
- No exceptions for standard pet requests.

## Guest Response Guidance

Respond firmly and politely. State no pets, no exceptions, mention the fine.
