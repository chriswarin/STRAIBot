## Policy or Fact

On Friday afternoons, landscapers and pool tech service the property, weather dependent.

- Friday afternoons: landscapers and pool tech visit to service the pool and property.
- If guests are in the pool at that time, they may be asked to exit briefly while work is performed.
- Service must be completed to maintain pool safety and cleanliness.

## Guest Response Guidance

Inform guests about Friday afternoon servicing and that they may be briefly asked to exit the pool.
