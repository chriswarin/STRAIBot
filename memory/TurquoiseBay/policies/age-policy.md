## Policy or Fact

Primary guest must be 25 years of age or older to book Turquoise Bay.

- Must be 25 to book.
- All guests under 25 must be related to a guest who is 25 or older.
- Chaperoned stays such as sports teams or organized groups are case by case.

## Guest Response Guidance

State 25+ requirement. Note under-25 guests welcome if related to a 25+ guest.

## Suggested Response

Hi! The primary guest at Turquoise Bay must be 25 or older to book. Guests under 25 are welcome when traveling with a direct family member who meets the age requirement.

## Escalation Notes

Chaperoned or organized under-25 groups require host review.
