## Policy or Fact

Turquoise Bay has a large private saltwater pool. Unheated. Open April through October.

- Large private saltwater pool for exclusive guest use.
- Unheated — no heating system, ambient/solar temperature only.
- Open April through October, may extend depending on weather.
- Pool is serviced Friday afternoons — guests may be briefly asked to exit.
- No glass containers in or around pool area. Use plastic or non-breakable containers.
- No running, diving, or horseplay.
- Rinse off sand and excessive sunblock before entering.
- Do not use pool if you have had diarrhea, stomach illness, or contagious illness in the past two weeks.
- Children under 12 must be supervised by a responsible adult at all times.
- Quiet hours 10pm–7am apply to pool area.
- Exit pool during thunderstorms or severe weather.
- Non-toilet-trained guests must wear swim diapers or rubber swim pants.
- Guests cover additional cleaning/draining/refilling costs if pool is soiled by bodily fluids or trash.

## Guest Response Guidance

Confirm large private saltwater pool, unheated, open April–October. Mention Friday servicing briefly. This is a high-priority retrieval scenario.
