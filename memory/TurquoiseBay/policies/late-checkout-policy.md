## Policy or Fact

Checkout at Turquoise Bay is strictly before 10:00 AM.

- No late checkout.
- Guests must be out before 10:00 AM because cleaners arrive at that time.
- $200 fee for a late checkout violation.
- The fee is NOT an approval to check out late — it is a penalty.

## Guest Response Guidance

State checkout is before 10am, cleaners arrive at 10am, $200 fee for violations.

## Suggested Response

Hi! Checkout at Turquoise Bay is before 10:00 AM — our cleaning team arrives right at that time and late checkout isn't available. A $200 fee applies for violations. Thanks for understanding!

## Escalation Notes

No escalation for standard late checkout requests.
