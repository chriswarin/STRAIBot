## Description
Blue Horizon is a modern mountain home on Massanutten's closest residential road to the slopes, lifts, lodge, and village. No single-family homes are located closer. The home has quick access to the resort's best attractions. It has been renovated with plank floors, comfortable beds, stylish furniture, updated toilets, an EV charger, and a 7-person hot tub. It includes fast WiFi, 4 TVs, games, a kids room with foosball and pinball, a grill, screen porch, fresh linens, towels, and easy no-chore checkout.
