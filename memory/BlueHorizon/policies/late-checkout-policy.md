## Policy or Fact

Checkout at Blue Horizon is strictly before 10:00 AM.

- No late checkout available.
- Guests must be fully out before 10:00 AM because cleaners arrive at that time.
- A $200 fee is charged for late checkout violations.
- The $200 fee is NOT an approval to check out late — it is a penalty.
- No exceptions, no extensions.

## Guest Response Guidance

State checkout is before 10am, cleaners arrive at 10am, late checkout is not available, and a $200 fee applies for violations. Never imply approval.

## Suggested Response

Hi! Checkout at Blue Horizon is before 10:00 AM — our cleaning team arrives right at that time and we're not able to offer late checkout. A $200 fee applies for late checkout violations. Thanks for understanding!

## Escalation Notes

No escalation needed for standard late checkout requests. If guest claims an emergency or special circumstance, escalate to host.
