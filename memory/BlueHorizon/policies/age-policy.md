## Policy or Fact

The primary guest booking Blue Horizon must be 25 years of age or older.

- Must be 25 to book.
- All guests under 25 must be related to a guest who is 25 or older.
- Chaperoned stays such as sports teams or organized groups are case by case.

## Guest Response Guidance

State the 25+ requirement. Note that under-25 guests are permitted if related to a 25+ guest. Escalate chaperoned group requests to host.

## Suggested Response

Hi! The primary guest at Blue Horizon must be 25 or older to book. Guests under 25 are welcome when they are traveling with a direct family member who meets the age requirement.

## Escalation Notes

Chaperoned groups, sports teams, or organized under-25 groups require host review (HostDecision).
