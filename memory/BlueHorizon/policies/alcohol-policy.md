## Policy or Fact

Open containers of alcohol are prohibited on any road or common area in the Massanutten community.

- No open containers of alcohol on roads or MPOA common areas.
- Alcohol consumption is permitted within the private property boundaries.
- This is an MPOA community rule.

## Guest Response Guidance

Hi! Just a heads up — MPOA rules prohibit open containers of alcohol on any road or common area in the community. Enjoy drinks on the private property, but please keep things contained when out and about. Thanks!
