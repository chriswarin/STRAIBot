## Policy or Fact

Cooking at Blue Horizon is welcome with restrictions.

- No heavy cooking.
- No aromatic foods.
- No gutting or cleaning fish, shellfish, or game animals inside the property.
- Everyday cooking (pasta, eggs, sandwiches, grilling) is fully welcome.
- Full kitchen available: GE stainless steel electric stove, oven, microwave, dishwasher, refrigerator, cookware.

## Guest Response Guidance

Confirm kitchen is available. State heavy aromatic cooking and fish/game cleaning inside are not permitted.

## Suggested Response

Hi! The full kitchen at Blue Horizon is ready for your use — stove, oven, microwave, dishwasher, grill, and full cookware. We just ask that guests avoid heavy aromatic cooking or cleaning fish/game inside the property. Everyday cooking is totally welcome!
