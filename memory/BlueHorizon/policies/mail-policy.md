## Policy or Fact

Do not send Amazon orders or any mail to Blue Horizon during your stay.

- Do not send Amazon or other mail deliveries to the property address.
- Mail may be put on hold at the post office and cannot be picked up by guests.
- The host does not receive mail at this address.

## Guest Response Guidance

Advise guests not to send any mail or packages to the property.

## Suggested Response

Hi! Please don't send Amazon orders or any mail to the Blue Horizon address during your stay — mail can get held at the post office and won't be accessible. If you need items shipped, please arrange delivery to your home before arrival or use a local service. Thanks!

No escalation needed.
