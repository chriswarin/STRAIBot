## Policy or Fact

All guests must review and agree to comply with the MPOA Rules and Regulations within 24 hours of booking.

- Within 24 hours of booking, guests must sign the MPOA agreement.
- Delay in submitting the signed agreement may result in cancellation.
- The MPOA agreement covers community rules including parking, noise, pool access, and resort conduct.

## Guest Response Guidance

Confirm the requirement and direct the guest to check email for the agreement link. Remind them it must be completed within 24 hours.

## Suggested Response

Hi! As part of your stay at Blue Horizon, all guests are required to review and sign the MPOA (Massanutten Property Owners Association) agreement within 24 hours of booking. You should have received a link — please check your email and complete it promptly. Let us know if you have any questions!
