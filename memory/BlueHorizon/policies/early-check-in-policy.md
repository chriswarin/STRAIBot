## Policy or Fact

Early check-in at Blue Horizon is subject to host availability and cleaning schedule. It is not guaranteed.

- Host should verify availability before approving.
- No automatic approval.
- Depends on prior guest checkout and cleaning team schedule.

## Guest Response Guidance

Acknowledge request, inform guest the host will review and follow up. Do not approve.
