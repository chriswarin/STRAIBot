## Policy or Fact

All trash must be taken to the community dumpster on checkout.

- Do not place trash anywhere outside the house, including screened porches.
- Trash attracts wildlife and may endanger guests and damage property.
- On checkout, all trash must be taken to the community dumpster one street over near Peak Dr — follow signs.

Instruct guests to take all trash to the community dumpster on checkout. Warn that trash outside attracts wildlife.
