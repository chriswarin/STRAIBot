## Policy or Fact

No pets are allowed at Blue Horizon under any circumstances.

- No pets of any kind.
- A $500 fine will be charged if evidence of pets is found on the property.
- No exceptions for standard pet requests.

## Guest Response Guidance

Respond firmly and politely. State no pets, no exceptions, and mention the fine. Do not suggest alternatives or loopholes.

## Suggested Response

Hi there! Unfortunately Blue Horizon does not allow pets of any kind — no exceptions. A $500 fine applies if evidence of a pet is found. We appreciate your understanding!
