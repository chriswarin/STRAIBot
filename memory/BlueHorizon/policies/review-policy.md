## Policy or Fact

Guests should have 2 or more positive reviews to book Blue Horizon.

- 2 or more positive reviews preferred/required.
- Case-by-case exceptions may be considered by the host.

## Guest Response Guidance

If a guest asks about a booking exception due to limited reviews, escalate to host. Do not auto-approve.

## Suggested Response

Thanks for reaching out! Booking review requirements are managed by the host directly. I've passed your message along and someone will follow up with you as soon as possible.

## Escalation Notes

RequiresHostReview: true, ShouldNotifyHost: true for any review exception request.
