## Policy or Fact

Blue Horizon has a free private parking garage. Maximum 3 cars.

- No more than 3 cars allowed.
- All cars must fit fully within the driveway with all tires off the roadway.
- Do not block fire hydrants.
- Do not park adjacent to neighboring homes or lots without explicit permission.
- Parking violators may be towed.
- Buses, campers, and RVs may not be parked at the house or along neighborhood roads.
- With prior MPOA approval, buses/RVs/campers may use the overflow lot.
- Overflow parking available at the MPOA Owners' Swimming Pool parking lot at 4007 Peak Drive.
