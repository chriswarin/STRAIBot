# BlueHorizon — Common Responses

## Ski Access
- Blue Horizon is on Massanutten's closest residential road to the slopes, lifts, lodge, and village
- No single-family homes are located closer to the resort
- This property is NOT ski-in ski-out — guests will need to drive or walk; quick access is available
- Do not describe the property as ski-in ski-out

## Hot Tub
- Yes, Blue Horizon has a private 7-person hot tub available for all guests
- It is cleaned between every stay
- Note: the hot tub may still be heating after arrival — allow time to reach full temperature

## Pets
- No pets are allowed at this property — no exceptions
- A $500 fine applies if evidence of a pet is found

## Smoking
- No smoking or vaping indoors or anywhere on the premises
- $500 fine per violation

## Parties and Occupancy
- No events, parties, or gatherings of any kind
- Maximum 14 guests on site at all times
- $500 fine per violation

## Parking
- Maximum 3 cars allowed; all must fit fully within the driveway
- Overflow parking at MPOA Owners' Swimming Pool lot at 4007 Peak Drive

## Late Checkout
- Late checkout is not available
- Checkout is 10:00 AM; cleaners arrive at that time
- $200 fee for violation — not an approval to stay late

## Coffee
- Drip coffee maker and Keurig both available

## WiFi
- Fast WiFi is included; credentials are in the welcome guide

## EV Charger
- EV charger available on request — Level 2, 220V

## Fireplace
- The fireplace is not usable

## Shampoo
- Shampoo is not provided; guests should bring their own

## Kids Room
- Kids room with foosball and pinball is available
- Pack-n-play / travel crib available on request
