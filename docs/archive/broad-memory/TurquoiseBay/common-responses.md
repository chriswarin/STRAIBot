# TurquoiseBay — Common Responses

## Beach Access
- Turquoise Bay is 1.5 blocks from the beach in Sandbridge
- Hot outdoor shower available for rinsing off

## Pool
- Large private saltwater pool — unheated
- Usually open April through October, may extend depending on weather
- Confirm current pool status with host if important for your stay

## Canal / Fishing / Kayak
- Canal waterfront area is not fully developed
- Guests can fish from the bank
- Guests may be able to launch a kayak with some effort

## Pets
- No pets allowed at this property — no exceptions
- $500 fine if evidence of pets is found

## Smoking
- No smoking or vaping indoors or anywhere on the premises
- $500 fine per violation

## Parties and Occupancy
- No events, parties, or gatherings of any kind
- Maximum 14 guests on property at all times
- $500 fine per violation

## Parking
- Maximum 4 cars allowed, all must fit fully within the driveway

## Late Checkout
- Late checkout is not available
- Checkout is 10:00 AM; cleaners arrive at that time
- $200 fee for violation — not an approval to stay late

## WiFi
- 1G WiFi is included; credentials are in the welcome guide

## Beach Equipment
- Some beach items may be available but are not guaranteed — confirm with host
- Bikes are not provided; local shops in Sandbridge offer rentals and may deliver
